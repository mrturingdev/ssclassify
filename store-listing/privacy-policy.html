S.S. Classify Privacy Policy

Effective October 6, 2026. Developer: Pushan Maharjan. Contact: pushan.maharjan3@gmail.com.

Your screenshots and the text in them never leave your device. Reading text (OCR), categorizing, titles and search all run on your phone. We have no account system, no ads and no tracking, and we never sell or share data for advertising.

What stays on your device

To organize your screenshots, the app reads them from your photo library with your permission, recognizes their text, and stores the results (text, titles, categories and your category corrections) in a database inside the app. None of this is uploaded. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

On supported Android phones, summaries can be produced by Google's on-device AI (Android AICore, Gemini Nano), which runs on the phone.

What is sent, and when

DataWhenWhat it contains
Crash reports On by default. Turn off anytime in Settings > Privacy. Technical details of the crash (error type, code location), app version, operating system version, device model, and a random ID created on install, so repeated crashes on one phone can be told apart from many phones. File names, paths, quoted values and long numbers are removed from error messages before sending. No screenshots, screen contents or text.
Anonymous quality stats Off unless you choose to share them, when asked or in Settings > Privacy. Coarse counts only: how many screenshots a scan analyzed (in ranges such as 11 to 100), how long it took (in ranges), how many could not be read, whether on-device AI was available, when you correct a category (which category to which), and when you use Re-analyze all. No ID is attached, so these stats cannot be connected to each other or to you.

Both are sent to Sentry (Functional Software, Inc.), which stores them in its European Union region and processes them only to provide this service to us. Sentry is configured not to store IP addresses. Data is kept for 30 days and then deleted. If you turn both settings off, the app stops reporting and deletes anything not yet sent.

Google ML Kit (Android only)

On Android, text and QR recognition use Google's ML Kit, which runs on the device. Google's ML Kit libraries send Google usage and performance metrics about these features: device information (manufacturer, model, OS version), the app's package name and version, a per-installation identifier, and performance and configuration data such as processing time and image size. According to Google, this does not include your images or their text. This collection is built into ML Kit and cannot be turned off in the app. See Google's ML Kit data disclosure. The iOS app uses Apple's on-device text recognition instead.

Permissions

Photo library access is used only to find and read your screenshots on the device. The app uses network access only for the reports described above.

Your choices

  • Turn crash reports and quality stats on or off in Settings > Privacy.
  • Because reports carry no account, name or contact details, we cannot look up data sent from your phone. Reports are deleted automatically after 30 days.
  • Deleting the app removes everything it stored on your device.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no personal information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published here with a new effective date, and any new kind of data collection will be explained in the app before it starts.
